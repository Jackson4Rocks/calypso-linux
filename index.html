01 · PHILOSOPHY

The system should
get out of your way.

Calypso is built around a simple idea: keep the system focused, the visual language coherent, and the choices understandable. Less noise. More intent.

01

Fast

Keep the experience responsive, direct, and free from unnecessary layers competing for attention.

02

Lean

A focused default system instead of a collection of software added simply to make the package list look bigger.

03

Intentional

Defaults, artwork, documentation, and interactions are chosen to feel like parts of one system.

04

Open

Source, configuration, artwork, and development live in public view so the project can be inspected and remixed.

02 · EXPERIENCE

Two ways to use
Calypso.

KDE Plasma is the main Calypso desktop. Hyprland stays available for people who prefer a more tiling, keyboard-driven workflow. The point is choice without forcing a single style on everyone.

04 · ARCHITECTURE

Architected
for clarity.

Calypso is more than a theme layered on top of Arch. The project is organized as recognizable layers: the Arch base, Calypso's system decisions, and the desktop/user experience built on top.

Arch baseRolling-release foundation and the package ecosystem Calypso builds upon.
Calypso systemDistribution defaults, branding, boot configuration, packages, live environment, and installer integration.
Desktop layerKDE Plasma at the center, with Hyprland configuration available as a separate workflow.
